# Spatial fractal discovery expedition

Started 20 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

The user's objective is a potentially new family or category of exactly self-similar spatial fractals, developed into a GitHub repository and Pages site with mathematically generated films, stills and definitions. A connected spatial object like Sixfold is sufficient; nonempty interior is not required. Rotational symmetry and strong visual character are preferences. The investigation is authorized to continue and use research agents.

## Acceptance criteria

- An explicit finite, contractive similarity IFS, or a clearly distinguished alternative with exact whole-in-part containment.
- Connectedness of the infinite limit, not merely overlapping rendered points.
- Full three-dimensional affine span.
- Exact symmetry claims distinguish rotation from roto-reflection.
- A structural result beyond a novel silhouette: contact geometry, topology of embedding, dimension behavior, or another defensible invariant.
- Novelty assessment against primary literature; no claim of historical priority from a search failing to find a match.
- Rendered geometry must derive from the same maps as the mathematical object. Thickness, finite sampling and camera adjustments must be disclosed.

## Current parallel work

1. Prior art: spatial tiles, embedded topology, IFStile and related constructions.
2. Constructive direction: symmetric sponges with rotation-dependent contact interfaces and controlled dimensions.
3. Survey direction: connected similarity attractors containing linked or knotted structures at every scale.
4. Root investigation: IFStile exploration, candidate comparison, independent review and rendering infrastructure.

The existing Sixfold, Upright, Porous Lantern and Helical Lace studies are references and remain separate. Their methods already include endpoint zippers, twisted necklaces, independently rotated edges, straight passage certificates, dimension bounds and repeated loop witnesses.

## Evidence levels

Keep analytic proofs, exact finite computations, floating-point screens and visual impressions separate. A candidate can be attractive and useful without being a new category. Publishing a candidate must state precisely which claims are established and which remain open.

## Early exclusions

Recursive Borromean imagery is prior art: Douglas G. Burkholder, *Brunnian Weavings*, Bridges 2010, and Richard Southwell's 2014 Borromean-rings-of-Borromean-rings construction. A new linked candidate needs a more specific contribution than recursive linking alone.

No discovery, exact topology classification, or publication has yet been claimed.
