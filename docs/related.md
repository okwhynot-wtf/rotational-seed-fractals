# Related work

This project investigates explicit combinations of known mechanisms. It does not claim to invent recursive links, similarity IFSs, overlapping fractals, or tangent-space theory.

## Similarity attractors

[Hutchinson, *Fractals and Self-Similarity* (1981)](https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/~john/Assets/Research%20Papers/fractals_self-similarity.pdf) provides the foundational compact-attractor framework. Preserving a seed by a subsystem is a direct use of this machinery.

[Ruan–Xiao, *The connectedness of Sierpiński sponges with rotational and reflectional components*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.09219) already treats rotated spatial sponge constructions. Rotating familiar cube patterns is not, by itself, a new category.

## Knots and links

[Fathauer's iterative fractal knots (2007)](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2007/bridges2007-335.pdf) and [Burkholder's *Brunnian Weavings* (2010)](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2010/bridges2010-263.pdf) are direct predecessors for recursive knot and Borromean imagery.

[Broden and collaborators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03639) establish that all knots embed in the Menger sponge. Arbitrary knot containment alone therefore does not distinguish a new family. Our comparison concerns the exact finite maps, retained graph, dimension bounds, and tangent behaviour together.

## Dimensions and tangents

[Fraser, Henderson, Olson and Robinson](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.1016) study Hausdorff–Assouad dimension gaps for overlapping self-similar sets. [García](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.03148) develops the spatial theory, including dense rotations and full Assouad dimension.

[Guido–Isola, *Tangential dimensions I*, Theorem 2.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305091) describes tangent spaces at generating fixed points through expanding inverse copies. The full-ball phenomenon here is an explicit instance of established fixed-point tangent theory.

[Käenmäki–Rutar, *Tangents of invariant sets*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.11971) establishes abundant large pointwise tangents for broad invariant-set classes. Our elementary proof verifies a particular stronger statement: all-scale convergence to a unique full ball at the stated centres.
