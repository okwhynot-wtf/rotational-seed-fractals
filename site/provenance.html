Provenance and reuse

Rotational Seed Fractals is an independent investigation by okwhynot-wtf, begun on 20 November 2026, Australia/Sydney. It follows earlier spatial-fractal investigations including Sixfold.

AI assistance

The work was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI GPT models through ChatGPT and Codex. Assistance included exploring constructions, developing mathematical arguments, searching primary literature, writing verification and rendering programs, and drafting documentation and this website.

The project owner initiated and directed the investigation and assessed its direction and visual results. Internal checks and separate-agent reviews are recorded in the repository. They are not independent external mathematical peer review. No historical-priority claim is established.

Research status

The paper draft, current results, prior-art audit, and internal review identify the supported claims and open questions. Exact overlaps, equivalence of different presentations, actual Hausdorff dimensions, and complement topology remain active topics.

Reports may evolve. A useful correction identifies the claim, source passage, and a reproducible computation or mathematical objection. Repository issues provide a public record for that discussion.
