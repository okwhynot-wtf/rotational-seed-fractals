Related work

This project investigates explicit combinations of known mechanisms. It does not claim to invent recursive links, similarity IFSs, overlapping fractals, or tangent-space theory.

Similarity attractors

Hutchinson, Fractals and Self-Similarity (1981) provides the foundational compact-attractor framework. Preserving a seed by a subsystem is a direct use of this machinery.

Ruan–Xiao, The connectedness of Sierpiński sponges with rotational and reflectional components already treats rotated spatial sponge constructions. Rotating familiar cube patterns is not, by itself, a new category.

Knots and links

Fathauer's iterative fractal knots (2007) and Burkholder's Brunnian Weavings (2010) are direct predecessors for recursive knot and Borromean imagery.

Broden and collaborators establish that all knots embed in the Menger sponge. Arbitrary knot containment alone therefore does not distinguish a new family. Our comparison concerns the exact finite maps, retained graph, dimension bounds, and tangent behaviour together.

Dimensions and tangents

Fraser, Henderson, Olson and Robinson study Hausdorff–Assouad dimension gaps for overlapping self-similar sets. García develops the spatial theory, including dense rotations and full Assouad dimension.

Guido–Isola, Tangential dimensions I, Theorem 2.6 describes tangent spaces at generating fixed points through expanding inverse copies. The full-ball phenomenon here is an explicit instance of established fixed-point tangent theory.

Käenmäki–Rutar, Tangents of invariant sets establishes abundant large pointwise tangents for broad invariant-set classes. Our elementary proof verifies a particular stronger statement: all-scale convergence to a unique full ball at the stated centres.

Current novelty status

The search did not locate the exact combined recipes and theorem stated in this project. Search absence is not evidence of priority. The appropriate description remains candidate families, with historical novelty unresolved.
